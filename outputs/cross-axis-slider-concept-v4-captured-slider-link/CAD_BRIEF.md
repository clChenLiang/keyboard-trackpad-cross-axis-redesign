# Cross-axis slider concept v4 — captured single-side linkage

- Base and motion: reuse the v3 compact 308 × 280 mm open base and all confirmed tray endpoint motions.
- Active linkage: left side only. The right side keeps the two tray guides but no duplicate active coupling.
- Keyboard driver: a red triangular bracket overlaps the keyboard side carriage at two attachment points and carries the shoulder shaft.
- Trackpad link: two blue slotted cheek plates capture the red driver from both X sides. Each cheek has a real through-slot and an independent roller on the common shoulder shaft.
- Trackpad attachment: each cheek uses two rigid necks plus two transverse tie pins through the trackpad side carriage.
- Axial stack: outer cheek → roller → spacer → red driver → spacer → roller → inner cheek, with 1.0 mm nominal cheek-to-driver gaps.
- Slot: 170 mm overall length, 7.2 mm width; roller OD 6.0 mm. Endpoint roller-center reserve is approximately 9.48 mm beyond commanded travel.
- Kinematics: slot axis remains derived from relative tray displacement (+145, +3) mm, not eyeballed.
- Validation: 201 sampled positions; zero computed cross-slot error; monotonic roller travel; unchanged tray endpoints; one active linkage only.
- Phase boundary: this is still a concept STEP. No final bearing selection, tolerance stack, strength, wear, friction, full interference, or manufacturing release is claimed.
