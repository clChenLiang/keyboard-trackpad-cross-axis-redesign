# V3 open-frame base — CAD brief

- New derivative; earlier V3 files remain untouched.
- FDM-oriented one-piece concept base, `310 × 225 × 6 mm`.
- Base top mounting datum remains at `Z=7 mm`; structural depth grows downward to `Z=1 mm`.
- Twelve-millimetre perimeter rails, three cross-members, four guide islands, and one widened right mechanism spine carry the fixed loads.
- A two-millimetre underside flange recess preserves the existing return-cartridge seating datum.
- Four primary TPU feet define `Z=-15 mm`; two auxiliary pads stop `0.3 mm` above that table plane.
- Scope excludes final insert sizing, material strength, tipping certification, print layout, STL/3MF, and continuous-pose analysis.
