- Independent v6 output; v1-v5 remain unchanged.

- Keyboard travel: +30 Y / -15 Z.
- Trackpad travel: -115 Y / -7.400872 Z.
- Two active captured linkages: left datum stack and right stack with 0.7 mm total X float.
