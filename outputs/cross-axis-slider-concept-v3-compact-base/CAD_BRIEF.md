# Cross-axis slider concept v3 — compact open base

- Scope: reduce the base footprint while preserving v1/v2 motion geometry.
- Versioning: v1 and v2 remain unchanged.
- Base envelope: 308 × 280 × 4 mm, reduced from 310 × 300 × 4 mm.
- Placement: front edge remains at Y = -115 mm; the rear edge moves from Y = 185 mm to Y = 165 mm.
- Side mechanism: fixed guide rails and their supports move inward 1 mm per side; tray, cross-axis pin, slotted link, return anchors, and endpoint motions remain unchanged.
- Lightening pattern: four rounded 110 × 110 mm through-windows with 10 mm radii.
- Remaining load paths: approximately 34 mm side rails, 20 mm longitudinal center rib, 26 mm transverse center rib, and 17 mm front/rear borders.
- Validation targets: base remains one connected solid; total static keyboard-mode footprint is 308 × 280 mm; all four endpoint displacement components remain unchanged.
- Phase boundary: no strength, stability, full interference, tolerance, or print validation is claimed.
