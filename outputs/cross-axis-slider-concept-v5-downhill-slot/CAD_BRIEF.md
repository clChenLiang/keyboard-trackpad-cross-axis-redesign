# Cross-axis slider concept v5 — 3° downhill captured slot

- Versioning: independent v5 output; v1-v4 sources and artifacts remain unchanged.
- Base: reuse the v3 compact 308 × 280 mm four-window open base.
- Keyboard motion: +30 mm Y, -15 mm Z, unchanged.
- Trackpad motion: -115 mm Y, -22.599 mm Z. This is 4.599 mm lower than v4.
- Active linkage: left side only; right side remains passive guidance.
- Straight slot: 3.000° relative-motion axis, derived from (+145, +7.599) mm rather than drawn by eye.
- Force path: the red keyboard driver carries a common shoulder shaft and two gold rollers; the rollers are captured between two blue slotted cheeks rigidly tied to the trackpad carriage.
- Keyboard guidance: each previous red two-roller pair is replaced by one neutral-gray 28 × 4.6 mm captured guide shoe. The long shoe preserves pitch constraint while avoiding false active-linkage nodes.
- Slot: 170 mm clear centerline length, 7.2 mm width; roller OD 6.0 mm.
- Phase boundary: concept STEP only. No final interference, tolerance, bearing, friction, wear, strength, print package, BOM, or manufacturing release is claimed.
